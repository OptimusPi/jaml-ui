## Priority Issues

### [P1] JSON-render shares `Apps/MCP on Desktop`
- **Why:** Storybook merges mcp.stories + jsonRender.stories. AI specs sit unlabeled next to JAML IDE; json-render skips `appMeta` JimboApp decorator.
- **Fix:** Own title, e.g. `Apps/MCP on Desktop/JSON-render` or a third Apps child. Use `appMeta("mcp")` or document the internal JimboApp.
- **Command:** `$impeccable clarify` / `$impeccable shape`

### [P1] Welcome and Foundations buried by A–Z
- **Why:** `storySort.order` only pins Primitives then Apps. Welcome is last Primitive. Foundations sit mid-list.
- **Fix:** Nested order: Welcome, Foundations, then buckets. Or hoist Welcome out of Primitives.
- **Command:** `$impeccable clarify`

### [P1] JimboDock (and JimboApp) have no leaf
- **Why:** Dock is SeedLab chrome; stories only show OuterTab “before they dock”. JimboApp variants named in Welcome, not clickable.
- **Fix:** `Primitives/Chrome/JimboDock` + a JimboApp shell story (or keep App as Apps-only and say so in Welcome).
- **Command:** `$impeccable shape`

### [P2] Layout junk drawer + parasite stories
- **Why:** 14 Layout leaves. Modal under InnerPanel, Spacer under Divider. Interaction/Menus are 1–2 item folders.
- **Fix:** Split Chrome / Navigation / Structure, or stop pretending folders are IA.
- **Command:** `$impeccable distill`

### [P2] Three wrap paths, no enforcement
- **Why:** StoryScene (34), appMeta (3), bare JimboApp (Panel, Wordmark), unframed Foundations + jsonRender.
- **Fix:** One primitive decorator; Apps only via appMeta; json-render on appMeta.
- **Command:** `$impeccable harden`
